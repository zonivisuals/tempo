What to look at

The indexing run advances on the 500 ms poll, so the step slide and the spinner are live. Drag the width to check the grid's caption clipping. A stage that has not started draws no row, so the run grows a row at a time instead of showing the whole pipeline up front — and the pill above it never moves: watch it hold still while the rows arrive underneath. Every indeterminate indicator is the accent arc over a white track. 02c is the reused-job case: every stage is done with total = 0, so no row may show a number. 02b is the synthetic queued row.
